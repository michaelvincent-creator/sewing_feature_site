The Anatomy of a Machine

Before you start stitching, let's get to know your new best friend! Modern machines might look scary, but they all share these core parts.

🎡 Hand Wheel

Manually raises and lowers the needle. Always turn it toward you!

🧵 Spool Pin

Holds your thread spool in place while you sew or wind a bobbin.

⚙️ Bobbin

The thread that comes from the bottom. It locks with the top thread to form a stitch.

🦶 Presser Foot

Holds the fabric flat against the feed dogs so it doesn't slip.

Essential Stitches

You don't need 100 fancy settings. 90% of sewing is done with just these two!

Straight Stitch

Used for seams, hems, and quilting. The bread and butter of sewing.

Zig-Zag Stitch

Stretches with fabric and finishes raw edges so they don't fray.

3 Golden Rules

  • Change your needle: If you hear a "thud-thud" sound, your needle is dull. Change it every 8 hours of sewing.
  • Iron as you go: Pressing your seams open makes the difference between "homemade" and "handmade."
  • Don't pull the fabric: Let the machine's "feed dogs" (the little teeth under the foot) pull the fabric. You just guide it!
